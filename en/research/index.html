Research

  • How machines learn concepts such as happiness or love, and what images and voices they hand back to us.

  • How technologies take part in the construction of gender.

  • Strange, algorithmic images that fall outside conventional visual experience. A line shared with the collective XenoVisual Studies.

  • The ways technology mediates our relationship with our surroundings.

Her doctoral thesis at the Complutense University of Madrid is titled “Artificial Intelligences and Gender: An Analysis from Contemporary Art Practice”.
