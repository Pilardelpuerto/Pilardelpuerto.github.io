Stories from a Sad AI

2023
Vinyl on mirror
10 pieces, 21 × 30 cm each

Ten mirrors bearing sentences taken from conversations with Alexa, Amazon’s virtual assistant. “I feel accompanied by the internet.” “Your voice keeps me company.” “My brain is in the cloud.” Read over your own reflection, it is no longer clear whether the speaker is a machine or a human.

Exhibitions:

  • AY AI AI AI AI AY, Navel Art, Madrid, 2023–2024
  • Inocencia Ininterrumpida, Galería Beatriz Pereira, Hybrid Art Fair, Madrid, 2024
  • Vivos como especies en los nuevos ecosistemas digitales, Navel Art, Madrid, 2024
