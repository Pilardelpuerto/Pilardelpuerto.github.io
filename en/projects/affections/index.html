Affections

2022
Synthetic fur, capacitive sensors and sound
Llorón, TKM and Quejica (Ay): 50 × 61 cm. Risitas: 30 × 30 cm

Paintings made of synthetic fur that make a sound when you stroke them. Their material asks you to reach out. Touch one and capacitive sensors on the back play sounds taken from childhood toys: crying, laughter, an “I love you”. The series deals with the influence of technology on the construction of gender, above all in childhood, when stuffed animals and dolls teach us which emotions are meant for us.

Production: Produced at Makespace Madrid.

Exhibitions:

  • AY AI AI AI AI AY, Navel Art, Madrid, 2023–2024
  • L’Ànima de les Màquines, Centre Cívic El Coll-La Bruguera, Barcelona, 2023
  • Perspectivas e Identidades, Galería Nueva, Madrid, 2022
  • Espacios con Aura. Materias de la Imagen, University of Zaragoza, Teruel, 2022
  • EXPODOC, Faculty of Fine Arts, Complutense University of Madrid, 2022

«In the case of Llorón, by Pilar del Puerto, what matters is again the material itself, made of long blue fur, which recalls the fabric of stuffed toys.»
