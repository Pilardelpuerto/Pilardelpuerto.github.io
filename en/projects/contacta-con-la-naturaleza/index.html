Get in Touch with Nature

2023
Interactive installation. Bug microphone and SIM card
Dimensions variable

A phone number for getting in touch with nature. You can call for free from your own phone and hear, live, the sound of a natural setting. The work runs both ways: the phone installed in the gallery also takes calls from the countryside. With irony, the piece points at our lack of connection with nature, and at how unsure we are of what that word means.
