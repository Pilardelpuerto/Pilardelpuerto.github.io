Controlling the Landscape

2020
Three glass cases filled with water
50 × 70 × 5 cm each

Three glass cases filled with water, hung on the wall like paintings. The water comes from three places in northern Extremadura and shows different levels of pollution; each case is titled with the coordinates of its source. The cases are not sealed: over time the water evaporates, leaving only layers of residue. A metaphor for a planet with an expiry date.
