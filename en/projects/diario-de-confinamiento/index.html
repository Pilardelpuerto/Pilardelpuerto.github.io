Lockdown Diary

2020
Photographic series. Print on wood and 4K video
13 pieces, 10 × 7 cm each. Video: 40 s

Thirteen photographs of the street taken from an intercom screen during lockdown. The street is Alberto Aguilera, in Madrid, seen from a rented flat. The screen, intensely blue, switches off after fifteen seconds. In a picture that would once have been full of people, what you see is the absence of bodies.
