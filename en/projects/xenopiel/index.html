Xenoskin

2023
Screen, AI-generated images, arcade claw, Arduino and electronics
Dimensions variable

An arcade claw tries, and fails, to grab the images on a screen. The screen plays an AI-generated video; the claw drops, misses and starts again, in an endless loop. The piece deals with the tension between desire and its fulfilment in the digital, and works with “xenoimages”: images that fall outside conventional visual experience.

Production: Xenovisual Studies (Mar Osés, Esther Rizo and Pilar del Puerto). Produced at Makespace Madrid.

Exhibitions:

  • AY AI AI AI AI AY, curated by Joel Blanco, Navel Art, Madrid, 2023–2024
