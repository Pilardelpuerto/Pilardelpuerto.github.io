The Holy Bible

2020
Looped video, clear plastic cushion and shredded Bible
50 × 50 cm

A shredded Bible inside a clear plastic cushion, alongside the video of its destruction. Shredding the book is an act of heresy, and carrying it out is a way of feeling the guilt particular to the Judeo-Christian tradition. The remains become a pillow, where dreams, guilt, fear and home meet, with the family as a disciplinary institution, as Foucault would have it.
