Love Is a Mystery to My Algorithm

2024
Sound installation. LED light, speaker, AI-generated text and synthetic voice
Dimensions variable

A sound installation in which a synthetic voice narrates a present-day romance. As it tells the story, the voice tries to picture “love” as an object of desire. The text was generated with AI. The piece asks how contemporary technology is reshaping the way we understand and live love.
