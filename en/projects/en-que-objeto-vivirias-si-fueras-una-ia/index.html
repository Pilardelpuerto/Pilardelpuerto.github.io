What Object Would You Live In If You Were an AI?

2023
Twentieth-century ceramic vase, cable and wireless speaker
20 × 35 × 17 cm

A twentieth-century ceramic vase with an artificial intelligence living inside it. From within, the AI tells in the first person how it escaped the “life of service” imposed on it by its condition and became a work of art. The piece is about agency and desire in non-human entities, and about what those desires say of our own concerns.
