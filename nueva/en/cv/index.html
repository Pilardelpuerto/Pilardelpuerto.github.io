CV

Pilar del Puerto (Plasencia, Spain, 1997) is an artist and researcher. Her work deals with artificial intelligence, gender and feelings. She has made a mirror that predicts your happiness, a chatbot that is sad, and a vase where an AI goes to hide.

She studied Fine Arts at the Complutense University of Madrid, where she is completing a PhD on artificial intelligence and gender in contemporary art. She co-founded the collective XenoVisual Studies. Her work has been shown in Spain, Canada, Croatia, Italy and Japan. She is represented by Galería Beatriz Pereira.
