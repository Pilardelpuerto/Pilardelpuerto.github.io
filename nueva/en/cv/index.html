Galería Beatriz Pereira

Pilar del Puerto is an artist and researcher. Her art practice and her academic research run in parallel and take on questions of social concern, from ecology to politics, always from a gender perspective. Her work sits close to conceptual art and treats each form a project takes as part of its conceptual material. At present she works on the changes technology brings to everyday doings, and on the effects it can have on the construction of gender.

Plasencia, Spain, 1997. Lives in Madrid. Fine Arts graduate and PhD candidate at the Complutense University of Madrid, with a thesis on artificial intelligence and gender. Co-founder of the collective XenoVisual Studies. Lecturer at Universidad Europea de Madrid and UOC. Her work has been shown in Spain, Canada, Croatia, Italy and Japan. Represented by Galería Beatriz Pereira.
