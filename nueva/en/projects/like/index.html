Like

2023
Paper tissues, ink and metal box
16 pieces, 24 × 12 × 7.5 cm each

Sixteen boxes of paper tissues printed with a heart and the word “like”. Tissues are for crying, or for looking as though you are. The installation deals with how emotions are performed on social media, and asks how much authenticity is left in a hyperconnected world.
