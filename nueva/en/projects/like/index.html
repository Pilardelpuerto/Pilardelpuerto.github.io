Pilar del Puerto

Like

2023
Paper tissues, ink and metal box
16 pieces, 24 × 12 × 7.5 cm each

Sixteen tissue boxes. Each tissue is printed with a heart and the word “like”. They are there for crying, or for pretending to, like so many feelings posted online.

Exhibitions:

  • AY AI AI AI AI AY, Navel Art, Madrid, 2023–2024
