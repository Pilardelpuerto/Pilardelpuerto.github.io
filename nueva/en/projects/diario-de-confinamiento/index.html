Lockdown Diary

2020
Photographic series. Print on wood and 4K video
13 pieces, 10 × 7 cm each. Video: 40 s

Thirteen photographs of Calle Alberto Aguilera in Madrid, taken during lockdown from the intercom screen of a rented flat. The picture comes up blue and switches off after fifteen seconds. It was the only window onto the street.
