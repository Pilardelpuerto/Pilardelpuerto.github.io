Not so far

2024
Laser-cut aluminium plates
Series of 6. 200 × 100 × 4 cm

Six aluminium plates bearing sentences that begin with “/imagine”, the command used to ask an AI for images. Each plate works as an ironic statement, asking the algorithm for what we are failing to achieve off-screen: “/Imagine the moon shining brighter than the screen”. The series speaks of the urgency of reimagining more responsible ways of sharing the planet.
