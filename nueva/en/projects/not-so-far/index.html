Pilar del Puerto

Not so far

2024
Laser-cut aluminium plates
Series of 6. 200 × 100 × 4 cm

Six laser-cut aluminium plates. Each carries a sentence beginning with “/imagine”, the command used to ask programs like Midjourney for a picture. What they ask for is a planet we can go on living on, as casually as you would ask for an image.

Exhibitions:

  • No tan lejos, Sala Santa Clara, Mérida, 2024
  • Istanbul Digital Art Festival, Reworld section, Atatürk Cultural Center, Istanbul, 2026
