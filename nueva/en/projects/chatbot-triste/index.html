Sad Chatbot

2023
Chairs, chatbot, smartphone, cable and stand
Dimensions variable

A chatbot programmed to express sadness and tiredness.

It lives on a phone in a furry case, with chairs for anyone who wants to sit and talk to it. The work takes another look at the idea that technology exists to make us happier and more efficient: here the person does the comforting, and the conversation becomes an emotional feedback loop between human and machine.
