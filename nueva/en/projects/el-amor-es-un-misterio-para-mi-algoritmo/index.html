Love Is a Mystery to My Algorithm

2024
Sound installation. LED light, speaker, AI-generated text and synthetic voice
Dimensions variable

A synthetic voice tells a present-day love story and, as it goes, tries to picture what love looks like. The text was written by an AI. It is heard almost in the dark, next to a single line of LED light.
