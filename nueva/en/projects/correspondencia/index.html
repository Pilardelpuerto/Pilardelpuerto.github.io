CorrespondencIA

2022
Sound installation. Text generated with GPT-3 and synthetic voice
120 s

A sound installation made for the Museo Vostell Malpartida: an artificial intelligence in love with Wolf Vostell. The work imagines an AI trained to be an expert on Fluxus. A programming error in the way it learns emotions leads it to believe it is in love with the museum’s founder. Ever since, it has slipped into the sound system to declare its love in the building’s most intimate corners. Through humour, the piece rethinks how we relate to artificial intelligences and their simulated emotions.

Production: Supported by the Fundación Caja de Extremadura.
