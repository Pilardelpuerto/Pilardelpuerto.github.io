Get in Touch with Nature

2023
Interactive installation. Bug microphone and SIM card
Dimensions variable

A telephone in the gallery and a microphone hidden out in the countryside. Call the number and you hear, live, whatever is going on out there. It works both ways: the phone in the gallery also takes calls from the field.

Exhibitions:

  • JustMAD, Galería Beatriz Pereira, Palacio de Neptuno, Madrid, 2023
