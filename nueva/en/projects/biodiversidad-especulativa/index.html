Speculative Biodiversity

2023
LED print on Fuji DPII Professional photographic paper, mounted on wooden stretcher
ILNI series #00–#06. 30 × 30 cm

Seven images of creatures that might exist, made in response to the loss of species caused by human activity. Shapes somewhere between animal, plant and light, titled ILNI and a number, from #00 to #06.

Exhibitions:

  • JustMAD, Galería Beatriz Pereira, Palacio de Neptuno, Madrid, 2023
