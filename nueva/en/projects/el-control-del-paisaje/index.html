Controlling the Landscape

2020
Three glass cases filled with water
50 × 70 × 5 cm each

Three glass cases hung like paintings, filled with water from three spots in northern Extremadura. You can see at a glance how polluted each one is. Each case is titled with the coordinates the water came from. They are not sealed, so the water slowly evaporates until only the residue is left.
