Xenoskin

2023
Screen, AI-generated images, arcade claw, Arduino and electronics
Dimensions variable

The claw from an arcade prize machine hangs over a screen lying on the floor. The screen plays an AI-generated video. Over and over, the claw drops down to grab the images and never manages it.

Production: Xenovisual Studies (Mar Osés, Esther Rizo and Pilar del Puerto). Produced at Makespace Madrid.

Exhibitions:

  • AY AI AI AI AI AY, curated by Joel Blanco, Navel Art, Madrid, 2023–2024
