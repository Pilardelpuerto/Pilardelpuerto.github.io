Pilar del Puerto

West

2017–2018
Ink on paper
Series of 13 pieces, 30 × 30 cm each

The train from Madrid to Plasencia takes over three hours, runs on a single unelectrified track and breaks down often. For this series, a simple machine inside the carriage turns the jolts of the journey into ink drawings. Each drawing is named after a stop. Near Madrid the line is fine; once the train enters Extremadura it turns rough. The project also includes photographs of the march for a decent train held in Madrid on 18 November 2018.
