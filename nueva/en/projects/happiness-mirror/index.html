Happiness Mirror

2023
Two-way mirror, AI software, camera, thermal printer and Raspberry Pi
70 × 70 × 10 cm

A mirror that predicts the greatest happiness you will reach in your lifetime. You stand in front of it, look at your reflection and press the button; a few seconds later the result comes out on a receipt. The prediction depends on how much or how little you resemble what the algorithm takes to be a man. The work grew out of a conversation with GPT-3, which stated that women never get to be as happy as men because of the discrimination they face. The receipt fades with time.

Production: Artistas en el Campus residency, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, with the MOBIOCHEM research group (Juan José Nogueira, Federico Ferlito, Marcos Domínguez Velad). Selected for Circuitos de Artes Plásticas, Comunidad de Madrid, 2024.
