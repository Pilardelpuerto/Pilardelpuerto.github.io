Happiness Mirror

2023
Two-way mirror, AI software, camera, thermal printer and Raspberry Pi
70 × 70 × 10 cm

A mirror that predicts how happy you will be over your lifetime. You look at yourself, press the button, and a thermal printer hands you a receipt with the result. The score depends on how closely you match what the algorithm takes to be a man. The idea came from a conversation with GPT-3, which said women are less happy than men because of the discrimination they face. The receipt fades over time.

Production: Artistas en el Campus residency, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, with the MOBIOCHEM research group (Juan José Nogueira, Federico Ferlito, Marcos Domínguez Velad). Selected for Circuitos de Artes Plásticas, Comunidad de Madrid, 2024.
