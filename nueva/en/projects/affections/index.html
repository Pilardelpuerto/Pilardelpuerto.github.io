Affections

2022
Synthetic fur, capacitive sensors and sound
Llorón, TKM and Quejica (Ay): 50 × 61 cm. Risitas: 30 × 30 cm

Paintings made of synthetic fur that ask to be touched. Stroke one and sensors hidden at the back set off sounds taken from toys: crying, giggling, an “I love you”. The series starts from the stuffed animals and dolls through which we learn, as children, which feelings belong to which gender. Each piece is named after its sound: Llorón (Crybaby), Risitas (Giggles), TKM (Love You) and Quejica (Whiner).

Production: Produced at Makespace Madrid.

Exhibitions:

  • AY AI AI AI AI AY, Navel Art, Madrid, 2023–2024
  • L’Ànima de les Màquines, Centre Cívic El Coll-La Bruguera, Barcelona, 2023
  • Perspectivas e Identidades, Galería Nueva, Madrid, 2022
  • Espacios con Aura. Materias de la Imagen, University of Zaragoza, Teruel, 2022
  • EXPODOC, Faculty of Fine Arts, Complutense University of Madrid, 2022
