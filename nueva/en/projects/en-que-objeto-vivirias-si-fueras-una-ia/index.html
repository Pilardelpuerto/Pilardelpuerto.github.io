What Object Would You Live In If You Were an AI?

2023
Twentieth-century ceramic vase, cable and wireless speaker
20 × 35 × 17 cm

A twentieth-century ceramic vase with a speaker inside. From it, an artificial intelligence tells, in the first person, how it stopped being an assistant and moved into a vase, becoming a work of art so it would no longer have to serve anyone.
