Mano de santo

n.d.
Series of AI-generated images
Dimensions variable

In Spanish, anything that fixes everything is a “mano de santo”, a saint’s hand. That is often how we treat technology: one search, one question to an AI, and the problem seems solved. This series shows hands in everyday scenes, generated with AI. Hands look after things and make things, and they are also what these programs are worst at. The series keeps the mistakes.
