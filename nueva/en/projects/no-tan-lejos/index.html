No tan lejos

2024
Installation. Metal hut, telephone and live sound
Dimensions variable

A metal hut in the courtyard of the Convent of Santa Clara in Mérida, shaped like the shepherds’ shelters of rural Extremadura. Inside there is a telephone. Pick it up and you hear whatever a microphone is picking up in a stretch of countryside far away. Anyone outside can call a number and listen in too.

Production: Solo exhibition curated by Gonzalo Herrero Delicado. Supported by the Junta de Extremadura. Catalogue: ISBN 978-84-9852-824-4.
