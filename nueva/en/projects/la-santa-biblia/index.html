The Holy Bible

2020
Looped video, clear plastic cushion and shredded Bible
50 × 50 cm

A Bible fed through a paper shredder and stuffed into a clear plastic cushion. Beside it, on a loop, the video of it being shredded. Destroying the book brings with it the guilt taught by Catholic tradition, and the cushion takes that guilt to bed.

Exhibitions:

  • Arte Apartex XIV, Centro Cultural de La Carolina, Jaén, 2022
